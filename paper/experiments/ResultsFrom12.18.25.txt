  | Dataset    | N    | s̄_within | s̄_across | Δ [95% CI]              | AUC [95% CI]         |                                                                  ──
  |------------|------|----------|----------|-------------------------|----------------------|

3. Exploratory Analysis: Granularity Mismatch & Density-Quality Curves                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                  
  This analysis examined how segmentation quality varies across different operating regimes (BOR = Boundary Oversegmentation Ratio).                              
                                                                                                                                                                  
  AUC Summary (Area Under Quality-Coverage Curves)                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                  
  | Dataset    | Model      | AUC (WF1) | AUC (Coverage) |                                                                                                        
  |------------|------------|-----------|----------------|                                                                                                        
  | DialSeg711 | neural     | 0.356     | 0.628          |                                                                                                        
  | DialSeg711 | texttiling | 0.305     | 0.609          |                                                                                                        
  | DialSeg711 | csm        | 0.322     | 0.630          |                                                                                                        
  | DialSeg711 | random     | 0.283     | 0.598          |                                                                                                        
  | SuperSeg   | neural     | 0.290     | 0.523          |                                                                                                        
  | SuperSeg   | texttiling | 0.229     | 0.497          |                                                                                                        
  | SuperSeg   | csm        | 0.207     | 0.477          |                                                                                                        
  | SuperSeg   | random     | 0.218     | 0.481          |                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                  
  Threshold Sweep Sample (Neural Model)                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                  
  DialSeg711 (sparse gold, ~2042 boundaries):                                                                                                                     
  - At BOR≈2.0: WF1=0.67, Coverage=0.65, Purity=0.91                                                                                                              
  - At BOR≈1.0: Higher precision but lower coverage                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                  
  SuperSeg (dense gold, ~2923 boundaries):                                                                                                                        
  - At BOR≈1.25: WF1=0.60, Coverage=0.76, Purity=0.87                                                                                                             
  - Naturally operates closer to BOR=1 due to dense annotations                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                  
  Key Findings                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                  
  1. No third regime found - datasets fall into two patterns:                                                                                                     
    - Sparse-gold (DialSeg711): Low boundary density, methods oversegment (BOR > 1)                                                                               
    - Dense-gold (SuperSeg): High boundary density, methods struggle to match density                                                                             
  2. Neural model outperforms baselines across both datasets in AUC metrics                                                                                       
  3. Granularity mismatch is dataset-dependent - the same model produces very different BOR values depending on annotation density

  1. BOR reveals granularity mismatch:                                                                                                                            
    - Undersegmenting (BOR < 1): SuperSeg (0.81), TIAGE (0.76), DailyDialog (0.91)                                                                                
    - Oversegmenting (BOR > 1): DialSeg711 (2.53), MultiWOZ (2.22), Taskmaster (5.23), Topical-Chat (3.91), QMSum (17.56)                                         
  2. Purity vs Coverage trade-off:                                                                                                                                
    - High BOR → High purity (~0.95+), lower coverage                                                                                                             
    - Low BOR → Lower purity, higher coverage (~0.90)                                                                                                             
  3. QMSum extreme case: BOR=17.56 indicates the model predicts 17x more boundaries than gold, likely due to meeting transcript domain mismatch
